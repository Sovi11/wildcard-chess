**Short description** (80 max)

**Full description** (4000 max)

    Hollow Chess is chess with one new rule: every third turn you may move a square of the board instead of a piece.

    Slide a square away and it leaves a hole. Rooks, bishops and queens cannot cross a hole. Knights jump over. Slide a square back in and a line opens. Kings get cut off, attacks appear from nowhere, and a pawn's promotion row can move away from it.

    The pieces move exactly as in chess, so you can play your first game in a minute.

    WHAT YOU CAN DO
    • Daily puzzle: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone, with a clock and a count of who solved it
    • 147 puzzles, each a forced mate with exactly one solution, checked by exhaustive search
    • Play the computer at five strengths, from Beginner to Brutal
    • Ranked games with a rating and a leaderboard
    • Play a friend online with one link, or two players on one phone
    • Sunday Arena: one hour of ranked games every week, most points wins
    • Review any finished game with an analysis of both sides' mistakes

    NO ADS, NO PURCHASES
    Hollow Chess is free. There are no ads and nothing to buy. You can play without an account; signing in keeps your rating and games across devices.

    The same game runs in the browser at hollowchess.com, with the same accounts.
