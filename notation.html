Hollow Chess Notation

Ordinary chess notation cannot say that a square moved. Hollow Chess Notation is long algebraic chess notation plus one new symbol, >, for moving a square.

Squares

Files are lettered a to h from left to right and ranks are numbered from 1, as in chess. Because the board can grow, a square attached above the eighth rank is on rank 9, and one attached below rank 1 is on rank 0. A file to the right of h is i, then j, and so on.

Piece moves

The starting square is always written. Short notation would be ambiguous on a board with holes.

NotationMeaning
e2-e4Pawn from e2 to e4. Pawns have no letter.
Nb1-c3Knight from b1 to c3.
Qd4xh8Queen captures on h8.
e5xd6 epEn passant capture.
O-O, O-O-OCastling kingside and queenside.
a7-a8=QPromotion, at the edge of the board wherever that is. e8-e9=Q is legal on a board that has grown.
+, #Check and checkmate, added to the end of a move.

Board moves

NotationMeaning
e5>c9The empty square e5 is lifted and attached at c9. e5 is now a hole.
g7>a9+A board move that gives check, usually by opening a line.
h3>f3#A board move that gives checkmate.

The rule of thumb: - and x move pieces, > moves the board.

Writing down a game

Moves are numbered in pairs, White then Black, exactly as in chess. Every third turn is a board turn, but the numbering ignores that. You can spot the board moves by the >.

1. e2-e4 d7>d9 2. Ng1-f3 e7-e6 3. d2>a9

Results are written 1-0, 0-1 or ½-½. In the game, the Copy button under the move list exports the whole score in this format.
