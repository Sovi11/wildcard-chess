# Hollow Chess — AI video prompt pack

For Veo 3 (Gemini), Sora, or Kling. Generate 9:16 vertical, 8s clips, then stitch
2–3 clips per short in CapCut. Veo 3 generates audio + dialogue from the prompt —
keep the QUOTED lines in the prompt so the trailer voice says them. End every
short with the logo card (brand/out/logo-1024.png) + hollowchess.com in CapCut.

Style keywords that keep the look consistent across clips — paste into every
prompt: "cinematic 3D render, Clash of Clans / Clash Royale trailer style,
dramatic rim lighting, embers and dust particles, deep blacks, gold accents,
9:16 vertical, epic orchestral hit, camera shake on impact".

## Assembly checklist (CapCut, per short)
1. Stitch clip A + B, trim dead frames at the joins.
2. Add end card: brand/out/logo-1024.png over #14161b, text "hollowchess.com —
   free in your browser", 2.5s.
3. If the generated VO is weak, mute it and layer the edge-tts lines from
   shorts/vo/ (already generated) or re-record with ElevenLabs.
