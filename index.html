Rules
  • Standard chess, local 2-player.
  • Your every 2nd move is a wildcard turn: move normally or Add / Remove / Shift.
  • Add a pawn on any empty square — even off the board, which grows it.
  • Remove any piece except a king.
  • Shift one of your pieces to any empty square.
  • Win by capturing the king. Red ring = a king is under attack.
