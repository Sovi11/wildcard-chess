Rules
  • Standard chess for the pieces — but the board itself is fair game.
  • Your every 2nd move is a wildcard: move a piece normally or reshape the board.
  • Add square — attach a new square anywhere touching the board.
  • Remove square — delete an empty square. The hole blocks sliding pieces.
  • Move square — pick up an empty square and re-attach it elsewhere.
  • Win by checkmate — on a wildcard turn you may escape check by reshaping the board (e.g. cut the attacker's line).
  • Pawns promote at the board's edge — extend the board and they must march further.
