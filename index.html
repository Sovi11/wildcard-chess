Rules
  • Standard chess for the pieces — but the board itself is fair game.
  • Every 3rd turn of the game is a board turn (W B✦ W B W✦ B W B✦ …): move a piece normally or move a square.
  • Click an empty square to pick it up, then click where to put it (anywhere edge-adjacent to the board — sharing a side, not just a corner). It leaves a hole, and holes block sliding pieces.
  • Win by checkmate — on a board turn you may escape check by reshaping the board (e.g. cut the attacker's line).
  • Pawns promote at the board's edge — extend the board and they must march further.

What is Hollow Chess?

Hollow Chess is a free chess variant where the board itself is a piece you can move. It plays by standard chess rules — same pieces, same moves, win by checkmate — with one addition: every third turn is a board turn. On a board turn you may move a piece as normal, or pick up an empty square and reattach it anywhere along the edge of the board.

The square you lift leaves a hole, and holes block sliding pieces. A bishop cannot see through one. Neither can a rook or a queen. So you can defend by deleting the ground an attack travels over, and escape check by reshaping the board instead of moving your king.

How is it different from normal chess?

  • The board is not fixed. It grows, shrinks and changes shape as you play.
  • Holes block lines. Bishops, rooks and queens cannot slide across a hole. Knights jump them.
  • Promotion moves. Pawns promote at the edge of the world — extend the board and they must march further.
  • Check has a second escape. On a board turn you can cut the attacker’s line rather than run.

Ways to play

  • Against bots — a ladder of opponents from beginner to brutal, no account needed.
  • Ranked online — matched against a player near your rating.
  • With a friend by link — send one link and play live in the browser.
  • Two players, one screen — offline hotseat.
  • Correspondence — trade links a move at a time.

Hollow Chess is free, runs in any browser on desktop or mobile, and needs no download and no sign-up. It installs as an app if you want it to.
