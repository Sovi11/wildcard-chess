Delete your Hollow Chess account

In the game (website or app)

  1. Open Hollow Chess and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile (the person icon at the top).
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

By email

Email hollowchess11@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password and display name
  • Your date of birth and chess level, if given
  • Your rating, game records and puzzle history

Nothing is kept afterwards. Anonymous usage statistics were never linked to your account, so there is nothing of yours left in them.
