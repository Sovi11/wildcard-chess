## r/chessvariants

Title: I built a chess variant where the board itself moves. There is now a daily puzzle

> Hollow Chess is normal chess with one change: every third turn you may move a square of the board instead of a piece. Holes block bishops, rooks and queens, kings can be cut off, and a pawn's promotion row can move away from it.
>
> I just added a daily puzzle. Everyone gets the same position, there is a clock, and you see how many people solved it and how fast. Every puzzle is a forced mate with exactly one solution, checked by exhaustive search, and in most of them the key move is a board move.
>
> https://hollowchess.com/daily
>
> It is free, runs in the browser, and needs no account. I would like to hear which puzzles feel unfair or too easy.

## Show HN

Title: Show HN: Hollow Chess, a chess variant where the board moves, with proved puzzles

> Hollow Chess is chess where every third turn you may move a square of the board. It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, and the bots run in the browser.
>
> The part I found interesting to build is the puzzles. There is no database of games to mine, so a composer places a few pieces on a board with holes and a solver keeps only positions where exactly one action forces mate at every step. There are 147 so far, and one is served each day to everyone: https://hollowchess.com/daily
>
> Source: https://github.com/Sovi11/wildcard-chess
