Privacy policy

Hollow Chess · website (hollowchess.com) and the Android and iOS apps · last updated 12 November 2026

Hollow Chess is a small independent game. It does not show ads, does not sell or share your data, and collects only what the game needs to work.

If you play without an account

  • Your rating, settings and game history are kept on your device only (browser storage or the app's storage).
  • Anonymous usage events, for example "a game started", "a game ended", how many moves were played and the screen size. These carry a random id that lasts for one visit and contain nothing that identifies you. They tell us where the game is confusing.
  • On the website, Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies.

If you create an account

  • Email address and password, used only to sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed by our database provider, Supabase.
  • Display name, shown on the leaderboard.
  • Date of birth and chess level, if you fill them in, used to pair you with suitable opponents.
  • Your rating, game records and puzzle attempts, so your progress follows you across devices.

We may email you about Hollow Chess, such as major updates or a request for feedback. Reply "stop" and we won't email you again.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the game, we store what you write and, only if you choose to give it, a way to contact you.

Playing online

Live games connect your device directly to your opponent's (peer-to-peer). A free connection service, PeerJS, introduces the two devices, and your IP address is visible to it and to your opponent's device, as with any direct connection. Moves are sent between the two devices, not stored on our servers.

Where the data lives

Account data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell it to anyone.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: sign in, open Profile, then tap Delete account. Your account, rating, games and puzzle history are erased immediately and permanently.
  • By email: write to hollowchess11@gmail.com from the address on the account, and we will delete it within 7 days.
  • Data kept only on your device is removed when you clear the site's data or uninstall the app.

Details: how to delete your account.

Children

Hollow Chess is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.
